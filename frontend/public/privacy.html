Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-27

MeshToMod turns a scan into a STEP file. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. Here is everything that happens to what you upload.

Your scan

When you open a mesh, the file is sent to our server to measure it and find the faces it can rest on. Nothing is stored for that step. When you convert, the scan is stored only while it waits and converts, and it is deleted as soon as the conversion finishes, fails or is stopped. A conversion is stopped if you cancel it, or if no page has checked on it for a few minutes (you closed the tab).

Your results

The STEP file, the model preview and the deviation map (which contains the scan's shape) are kept for one hour so you can download them, then deleted. They can only be reached with the random link the app gets back; we don't list, share, sell or look at them, and we never use them to train AI models.

Your address

To keep the free service fair, the server counts conversions per IP address. That count is held in memory for up to an hour and is not written to disk. We don't keep access logs.

In your browser

The app remembers your units and up-axis choices in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. It also keeps the random link to your latest conversion there, for up to two hours, so a reloaded or reopened page can show its progress or its result (which the server still deletes after one hour). Opening another mesh clears it.

Questions

MeshToMod is open source (source code), so you can check all of this, or run it on your own computer instead.
